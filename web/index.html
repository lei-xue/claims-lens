Claims Lens: every denied dollar, worked to a finish

Claims Lens is a denial-management work queue for US medical practices. It shows what insurers didn't pay, why, and what to do next, so a billing team recovers the money before the appeal deadline.

  • Reconciliation: imports insurer payment files (X12 835 remittances) and matches each payment to the claim the practice sent (X12 837P), line by line: billed, allowed, paid and every adjustment.
  • Work queue: every denial becomes a task with a plain-English reason, the denied amount and the appeal deadline; overdue items come first.
  • Plain-English denial codes: each claim adjustment reason code (CARC) and remark code (RARC) is explained with common causes and the next step.
  • Closed loop: fix and resubmit a corrected claim or draft an appeal; when the next payment arrives, the denial closes itself and the recovery is counted.
